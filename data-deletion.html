Data Deletion Request — ouiShare

Last updated: June 2026
Applies to: ouiShare mobile application (Android and iOS)
Developer: McCleery Enterprises

You have the right to request deletion of your ouiShare account and all personal data associated with it. This page explains how to make that request, what will be deleted, and what may be retained.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to tyrtel@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: ouiShare — Account Deletion Request
  3. Include the email address or name associated with your ouiShare account so we can identify your data
  4. We will confirm receipt within 5 business days and complete deletion within 30 days

What Gets Deleted

Data typeActionTimeframe
Account (name, email, user ID)Permanently deletedWithin 30 days of request
Trips and expenses you createdPermanently deletedWithin 30 days of request
Participant records linking you to tripsPermanently deletedWithin 30 days of request
Receipt images you uploadedPermanently deletedWithin 30 days of request
Payment status records within ouiSharePermanently deletedWithin 30 days of request

What May Be Retained

Data typeReason retainedRetention period
Stripe payment transaction recordsLegal and tax obligations (EU financial regulations)7 years from transaction date
Anonymised aggregate usage data (if any)Cannot be linked back to youIndefinite

Note on contacts: ouiShare reads your device contacts locally to help you add participants. Contact data is never uploaded to our servers, so there is nothing to delete on our end.

Note on third-party processors: Deletion of your ouiShare account does not automatically delete data held by Stripe or Tink under their own privacy policies. If you wish to request deletion from those services, please contact them directly:

  • Stripe: stripe.com/privacy
  • Tink: tink.com/privacy-policy

Questions

If you have any questions about this process or your data rights under GDPR, contact us at tyrtel@gmail.com.
